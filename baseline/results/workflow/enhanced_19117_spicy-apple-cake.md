## Recipe

### Ingredients

- 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 0.5 teaspoon baking soda
- 0.5 teaspoon salt
- 0.5 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 0.5 cup shortening
- 0.25 cup white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 0.5 cup milk
- 2 cups apple - peeled, cored, and chopped
- 2 cups sifted confectioners' sugar
- 0.25 cup shortening
- 0.25 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 pinch ground nutmeg
- 0.25 cup evaporated milk

### Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease and flour a 9-inch square pan.
2. Sift together flour, 1 teaspoon cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and nutmeg. Set aside.
3. Cream brown sugar, 1/2 cup shortening, and white sugar together in a large bowl until light and fluffy. Beat in eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla. Beat in flour mixture alternately with milk, mixing until just combined. Stir in chopped apples.
4. Spread batter evenly in the prepared pan. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center of cake comes out clean, 35 to 40 minutes. Let cool.
5. Combine confectioners' sugar, 1/4 cup shortening, 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon, and nutmeg in a medium bowl. Beat until frosting is light and creamy. Beat in evaporated milk 1 tablespoon at a time until desired spreading consistency is reached. Spread over top of cooled cake.
