## Recipe

### Ingredients

- 1 cup butter, softened
- 0.5 cup white sugar
- 1.5 cups packed brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 egg yolk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon cream of tartar
- 1 dash cinnamon
- 2.5 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips

### Instructions

1. Gather your ingredients, making sure your butter is softened, and your eggs are room temperature.
2. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Beat butter, white sugar, and brown sugar with an electric mixer in a large bowl until smooth.
3. Beat in eggs and the egg yolk, one at a time, then stir in vanilla.
4. Add baking soda to batter along with salt and cream of tartar.
5. Stir in flour, cinnamon, and chocolate chips.
6. Cover and refrigerate the dough for at least 1 hour.
7. Drop scoops of dough (an ice cream scoop makes 16 big cookies) 2 inches apart onto ungreased baking sheets and press them down slightly.
8. Bake in the preheated oven until edges are nicely browned, about 10 minutes.
9. Cool on the baking sheets briefly before removing to a wire rack to cool completely.
10. Store in an airtight container or serve immediately and enjoy!
